## Terminology decisions

The following terminology was intentionally preserved without modification:

- `SEC701-0000029`: Black-box, gray-box, and white-box testing
- `SEC701-0000139`: Information rights management
- `SEC701-0000149`: Purple-team exercise
- `SEC701-0000200`: White-box testing

The following questions were revised so the learner is tested on the Security+ concept rather than being required to recognize a more specialized label:

- `SEC701-0000087`: Replaced decompose, monolithic, east-west, and distributed-transactions wording with plain-language microservices wording.
- `SEC701-0000088`: Replaced bastion host with CompTIA-aligned plain-language jump-server terminology.
- `SEC701-0000095`: Tested safe retry behavior without requiring the learner to know the term idempotent.
- `SEC701-0000113`: Replaced pseudonymization recognition with the CompTIA-listed tokenization concept.
- `SEC701-0000147`: Replaced vendor concentration risk as required vocabulary with a plain-language dependency description.
- `SEC701-0000172`: Replaced fourth-party risk as required vocabulary with a direct subcontractor-risk description.
- `SEC701-0000193`: Replaced SCIM recognition with the broader automated identity lifecycle capability; SCIM remains in the explanation.
- `SEC701-0000202`: Replaced M-of-N terminology with a direct multiple-custodian requirement while retaining dual control in the explanation.
- `SEC701-0000207`: Replaced BOLA as required vocabulary with a direct description of the missing object authorization check.

Specialized labels such as idempotency, vendor concentration risk, fourth-party risk, SCIM, and BOLA may still appear in explanations or study metadata when they help teach the industry term. They are no longer required to select the answer.
